Replaced by `measureClaimAttestation(question, items, language)`, two independent deterministic signals over the **best single retrieved card**: (a) `lexical` — unordered, prefix-tolerant bag coverage of the question's content words after removing interrogative/reporting/speech-act scaffolding (`ASK_FRAME_TERMS`), and (b) `aligned` — the project's own order-sensitive `alignWordsDP` (corpus-derived word similarity) over the full and de-duplicated source token lists. The referral path, the `items.length === 0` / `topScore < 15` short-circuits and the `isRulingQ` branch are unchanged.
